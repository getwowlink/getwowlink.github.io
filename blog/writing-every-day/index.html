How we write a little every day

Oct 2, 2026 · Lumen team

Five minutes, one note, no streaks to break. The small habit behind every good idea we've shipped.

We don't believe in streaks. Streaks turn a pleasant habit into a chore you can fail at.

Instead, Lumen opens on a blank note with today's date. Write one line or a page; either counts.

After a month you have thirty small notes, and a few of them are the start of something bigger.
