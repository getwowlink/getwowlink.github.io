Why Lumen works offline first

Sep 18, 2026 · Lumen team

Your notes live on your device and sync when they can. Here's how we built it and why it matters on a plane.

Every note is saved on your device before anything is sent anywhere.

When you're back online, changes merge quietly in the background, even if you edited the same note on two devices.

The result: Lumen opens instantly, on a plane, in a tunnel, or on hotel Wi-Fi.
